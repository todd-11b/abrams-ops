# Software delivery operating contract

**Owner decision: Todd, October 18, 2026. Applies to authorized software work across his projects.**

Todd directed: “Go ahead and put this in place across all my projects. Adapt Superpowers, fix the GitHub safeguards, and handle everything through verified deployment. Only bring me decisions that actually need me.” He explicitly included brainstorming and planning.

## Authority and supersession

Approval of an outcome and its business limits authorizes its routine technical delivery: investigate, design, plan, implement, test, obtain independent review, push, merge and follow the established production deployment. The delivering agent owns that work; Todd does not select engineering tactics, carry review messages or click Merge.

This expressly supersedes older requirements for a separate mechanical merge/deploy yes, Cursor-only merge authority, mandatory plan/execution-method menus, and stopping at PR creation. In Barley Bus governance it is a narrow amendment to Constitution rules 3 and 7 and the mechanical shipping parts of DEC-033/DEC-038 and Operator Mode. Their business/data safety limits remain. Historical decisions and system laws are preserved.

This delegation does not authorize unrelated open PRs, new business policy, unapproved financial commitments, new customer promises or messages, widened access, irreversible/destructive actions, or production data repairs. Production SQL/migrations retain applicable scoped approval requirements. A specific recorded authorization can cover an exact reviewed migration; an agent's belief that SQL is safe cannot create authority. Research publication does not authorize implementing its commercial recommendations. Existing account-access and preservation restrictions remain.

## Brainstorm, plan and deliver

For a new or unclear outcome, inspect the current system, consider useful alternatives and recommend the smallest complete design. Ask Todd only about material intent, business consequences or authority. For a clear small fix, a short approach in the existing work record is enough. Reuse an approved plan instead of restarting discovery.

For substantial work, keep a concise recoverable plan: intended behavior, affected components, dependencies, verification, release order and recovery limits. The agent chooses technical execution and continues without another plan handoff when the outcome is already approved.

Reuse the existing task, branch and PR. Check current source and active ownership before acting. Trace failures to their causes, preserve canonical architecture, and add meaningful regressions. Run relevant checks and required repository checks; distinguish passing evidence from untested behavior and known baseline limitations.

A second agent or reviewer reads the final diff independently. Fix findings on the same branch; material fixes receive a scoped independent read on the resulting final head. Record reviewer, exact head and findings in the existing PR record. Shared account attribution is procedural, not proof of separate authenticated identities. Do not manufacture self-approval or a status claiming independent review.

Before merging, verify the current head, dependencies, required checks and blocking findings. Use the platform's expected-head precondition. Do not disable or bypass protection to merge. Follow the established deployment once, inspect its actual result and released commit, then verify the original user outcome. A passing test, successful build, HTTP 200 or deployment readiness alone does not prove an untested business flow.

On failure, inspect evidence and reconcile partial completion before retrying. Automatic rollback requires established data/schema/external-effect compatibility; otherwise contain the failure and choose an authorized forward repair. Preserve release evidence and unique work. If proof requires a future natural event, retain a finite pending verification with an owner, event and deadline.

## Enforced checks and cost discipline

Use one stable `delivery-checks` result on every PR; required contexts must never disappear because of a workflow path filter. Checks run without production/provider secrets. Bound native Actions runtime, cancel superseded verification, reuse locked dependencies and run product checks in proportion to changes. This adoption authorizes those bounded verification jobs; it creates no recurring model supervisor, paid service subscription or scheduled product mutation.

Keep PR-only integration, protected history and resolved blocking review discussions. Require checks from the intended GitHub integration and remove routine bypass. Enforce meaningful passing checks only after proving their first run; do not configure a nonexistent context or revive retired checks. Repository-specific commands and hosting remain in that repository.

## Completion and maintenance

The delivering agent records the reviewed/released commit, actual check and deployment evidence, original-outcome verification and material limits on the same work item, then briefs Todd concisely. Keep independently actionable work moving while a genuine owner decision is pending.

Its methods are informed by [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), whose upstream license is MIT; its generic human merge menu, automatic setup/cleanup and rigid per-task agent choreography are replaced by this contract. Question a method when evidence shows it is unsuitable.
